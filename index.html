AllerScan Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Overview

AllerScan is an app developed by Gimer Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use AllerScan, including information stored on your device and information processed by third-party services. AllerScan does not require an account.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

AllerScan can store your selected allergens and other app preferences locally on your device so that the app can compare product ingredients against your selected allergens.

These locally stored preferences are used to provide the app's core functionality. AllerScan does not intentionally send your saved allergen preferences or scan history to any external service for processing.

The information stored locally may be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. (May depend on device and operating system.)

3. Third-Party Services and Internet Requests

AllerScan uses third-party services to provide certain features. When you use these features, information may be sent to the relevant service as described below.

Product Information: Open Food Facts

AllerScan uses Open Food Facts to retrieve product information.

Open Food Facts processes requests according to its own policies. For more information, visit Open Food Facts.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

AllerScan uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information to deliver advertisements, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and maintain the security of their services.

Depending on the services used, your device settings, your location, and applicable requirements, information processed by advertising services may include:

  • IP address
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where available.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and interactions with advertisements.
  • App interactions relevant to ad delivery and measurement.
  • Diagnostics, performance information, and fraud-prevention data.

The information actually processed depends on the advertising SDK configuration, the type of advertisement, and the choices available to you. AllerScan does not intentionally provide your saved allergen preferences or scan history to AdMob for personalized advertising.

Google may process information in accordance with its own policies. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies.

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, AllerScan may display a consent message that allows you to review and manage choices related to advertising and the processing of information for advertising purposes.

The types of advertisements that can be requested or displayed may depend on your consent choices, device settings, and applicable laws.

You can also review available advertising privacy controls through your device settings and Google's privacy resources. For more information, visit Google My Ad Center. Available controls vary by device, region, and service.

6. In-App Purchases and Remove Ads

AllerScan offers an optional in-app purchase that removes advertisements. Purchases and purchase restoration are handled through the relevant app store, such as Apple's App Store or Google Play.

The app processes purchase status to determine whether advertisements should be displayed. The app stores may process purchase and transaction information under their own policies. AllerScan does not receive your full payment card details through the normal in-app purchase process.

When the Remove Ads entitlement is active, AllerScan is designed not to request or display its normal in-app advertisements.

7. Children's Privacy

AllerScan may be used by children and general audiences. We recognize the importance of protecting children's privacy and complying with applicable children's privacy laws and app-store requirements.

AllerScan does not require users to create an account or intentionally ask children to submit personal information directly. However, the app uses third-party services, including advertising services, that may process device or connection information as described in this policy.

Advertising and consent settings must comply with applicable age-related advertising requirements. The data handling described in this policy also applies when a child uses the app, subject to any additional protections required by law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by AllerScan generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, depending on the operating system.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to those providers practices. AllerScan cannot directly control information retained independently by those providers.

9. Security

AllerScan relies on device security features to protect locally stored information. Information sent to third-party services is subject to the security practices of those services.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have rights relating to consent and targeted advertising.

To submit a privacy question or request, contact us using the email below. Requests concerning information held independently by a third-party provider may need to be directed to the specific provider.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
