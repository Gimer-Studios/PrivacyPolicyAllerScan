AllerScan Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2025

1. Overview

AllerScan is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what data is handled by the app, how it’s stored, and your rights. Allerscan complies with Google Play’s requirements for apps intended for children and general audiences.

2. Data Collection and Storage

AllerScan does not collect or transmit any personal or sensitive user data to external servers. All data generated or used by the app is stored locally on your device.

3. Use of Data

Locally stored data is used solely to provide app functionality and improve your experience. Allerscan does not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

4. Children’s Privacy

AllerScan is suitable for users aged 3 and up. While the app requires an internet connection for certain features to function, it does not collect personal information from children, nor does it transmit data to external servers or third parties. The app does not include account creation, online chat, or external data sharing. All data remains stored locally on the device.

5. User Control

You have full control over your data. You can delete app data at any time by clearing the app’s storage through your device settings. No login or account is required, and no data is stored outside your device.

6. Security

Since all data remains on your device, AllerScan relies on your device’s built-in security features. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app’s functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and notify users through the app or store listing.
